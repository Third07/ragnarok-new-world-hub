How to use the Ragnarok: The New World World Map

Select Open World or Instances / Dungeons, choose a map, then toggle marker categories for monsters, quests, chests, landmarks, recipes, and NPC services. Tap a marker to open its details, use pinch or the compact zoom controls on mobile, and enable marker labels only when you need them. Monster and card links help turn a map discovery into a practical farming route.

Find butterflies, bubbles, sun chests, snow, weather monster chests, and season chests in the filters. These are recorded locations, not live spawns; availability can depend on weather, season, and game progress. Counts show mapped locations, so some areas may be incomplete. Tap a weather or photography marker to mark it complete, then use Hide checked. Checks are saved in this browser.

SEA locations and exploration data: RoworldDB. The SEA data is checked during site builds; failed downloads keep the last validated data.
