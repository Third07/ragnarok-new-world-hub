How to use the Ragnarok: The New World interactive map

Select a Rune-Midgard map, then toggle marker categories for monsters, quests, chests, landmarks, recipes, and NPC services. Tap a marker to open its details, use the large zoom controls on mobile, and reset the view whenever you need to recenter. Monster and card links help turn a map discovery into a practical farming route.
